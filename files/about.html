About DeltaIQ

DeltaIQ is operated by DeltaIQ, LLC — a publisher of rules-based, observational analysis of SPY and SPX market conditions. We are not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or financial adviser. Content is informational and educational only.

Founder

Timothy Ong

Founder, DeltaIQ

Timothy Ong is the founder of DeltaIQ. He spent over a decade in revenue and sales operations — most recently at Crusoe & Angi — building the systematic, data-driven frameworks that now power DeltaIQ’s rules-based approach to SPY/SPX market-condition analysis.

Timothy Ong founded DeltaIQ to bring the same rigor used in enterprise analytics to short-dated index options. With an MBA from the University of Arizona and a BS in Finance from Purdue, Timothy’s background spans revenue operations and analytics roles at companies including Angi, DISH Network, BCG, and Vantiv — work centered on building defensible, data-backed systems rather than gut-feel decisions. DeltaIQ applies that same discipline: a rules-based, backtested framework that publishes the same observational analysis to every subscriber, with no discretionary calls and no personalized advice.

Why it exists

Why we built this

Intraday option market structure moves quickly. Most “signal” products blur observation with advice, or bury the reader in noise. DeltaIQ was built to do one job clearly: apply a fixed, automated method to observable SPY/SPX conditions and publish the same observational read to every subscriber at the same time.

  • Identical publication for all subscribers — no personalized recommendations
  • Full analytical detail on the subscriber dashboard; optional SMS only prompts you to sign in
  • Rules-based automation with no discretionary overrides to publication output

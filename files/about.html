About

Who publishes DeltaIQ

DeltaIQ is operated by DeltaIQ, LLC — a publisher of rules-based, observational analysis of SPY and SPX market conditions. We are not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or financial adviser. Content is informational and educational only.

Why it exists

Why we built this

Intraday option market structure moves quickly. Most “signal” products blur observation with advice, or bury the reader in noise. DeltaIQ was built to do one job clearly: apply a fixed, automated method to observable SPY/SPX conditions and publish the same observational read to every subscriber at the same time.

  • Identical publication for all subscribers — no personalized recommendations
  • Full analytical detail on the subscriber dashboard; optional SMS only prompts you to sign in
  • Rules-based automation with no discretionary overrides to publication output

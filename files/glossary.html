Publication Glossary

DeltaIQ publishes observational content on your Analytics Dashboard (sign in via My Account). This page explains the dashboard publication format. Optional text messages are a separate sign-in reminder only — they never include the publication body below.

Publications describe market conditions and do not guarantee results. Informational only — not personalized investment advice.

Optional text Dashboard example Bull vs Bear Line by line Reminders

01

Optional text notification

If you opt in, texts only prompt you to sign in when new publications are available. Text messages do not include publication details.

02

What you see on the dashboard

When defined market conditions occur, subscribers see a publication on the Analytics Dashboard, presented in a three-line format. See below for an example.

03

Bull vs Bear

The dashboard headline uses one of two publication labels for the market condition set. Neither is a prediction or an instruction to buy or sell.

SPY | Bull conditions aligned

Market conditions under the model’s rules are labeled bullish for that publication cycle. Context only — not a call to trade.

SPY | Bear conditions aligned

Market conditions under the model’s rules are labeled bearish for that publication cycle. Context only — not a call to trade.

04

Line by line (dashboard)

Each dashboard publication has three lines and nothing else.

What you see
What it means
SPY | Bull/Bear conditions aligned
Headline with the publication direction label for the market condition set.
Current SPY $…
SPY price snapshot at the moment of publication. Observational context only.
Observational conditions only…
Reminder that the publication describes market conditions and is not a trade recommendation.

Publications do not specify entry price, exit price, strike, expiration, contract size, position size, or stop-loss levels.

05

A couple of reminders

  • Bull / Bear are dashboard publication labels — not predictions or trade instructions.
  • Optional SMS frequency varies with how often the dashboard is updated. There is no fixed count per day.
  • Dashboard publications and optional texts are informational and not personalized financial advice. You alone decide what, if anything, to do.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of SMS. Opting out stops text notifications but does not by itself cancel billing — use My Account to manage your subscription.

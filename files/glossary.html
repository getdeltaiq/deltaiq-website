Alert Glossary

Every DeltaIQ text uses the same MARKET format. This page explains what you receive and how to read it.

Alerts describe observed conditions and do not guarantee results. Informational only — not personalized investment advice.

Example text Bull vs Bear Line by line Reminders

01

What a text looks like

Subscribers receive one standardized MARKET alert whenever defined conditions are observed. Multiple internal rules may contribute; the text you get is always the same three-line format.

02

Bull vs Bear

The headline uses one of two publication labels for the observed condition set. Neither is a prediction or an instruction to buy or sell.

SPY | Bull conditions aligned

Observed conditions under the model’s rules are labeled bullish for that publication cycle. Context only — not a call to trade.

SPY | Bear conditions aligned

Observed conditions under the model’s rules are labeled bearish for that publication cycle. Context only — not a call to trade.

03

Line by line

Each text has three lines and nothing else.

What you see
What it means
SPY | Bull/Bear conditions aligned
Headline with the publication direction label for the observed condition set.
Current SPY $…
SPY price snapshot at the moment the alert was sent. Observational context only.
Observational conditions only…
Reminder that the alert describes observed conditions and is not a trade recommendation.

Alerts do not specify entry price, exit price, strike, expiration, contract size, position size, or stop-loss levels.

04

A couple of reminders

  • Bull / Bear are publication labels — not predictions or trade instructions.
  • Message frequency varies with market activity. There is no fixed count per day.
  • Subscriber texts are not sent overnight after the late-day quiet window through the next regular-session open.
  • These alerts are informational and not personalized financial advice. You alone decide what, if anything, to do.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of SMS. Opting out of texts stops alert delivery but does not by itself cancel billing — use My Account to manage your subscription.

Consent

Text notifications — optional SMS

SMS is optional and separate from purchase. You can subscribe and use the subscriber dashboard without texting. If you opt in (separate checkbox at checkout, unchecked by default), you may receive recurring automated texts that notify you when new subscriber dashboard content is available and prompt you to sign in at My Account. Text messages do not include market, ticker, price, or publication detail. Message frequency varies with how often we publish new dashboard content. Message and data rates may apply. No mobile information or SMS consent is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at support@getdeltaiq.com.
  • Declining SMS does not block checkout or dashboard access. Opting out of SMS stops texts but does not by itself cancel your subscription or billing. You can enable SMS later in My Account.
  • See our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Important Disclosures for more information.
  • Carrier-review evidence (step-by-step flow, hosted screenshots, sample messages): getdeltaiq.com/sms-opt-in/.

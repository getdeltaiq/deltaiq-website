DeltaIQ optional SMS opt-in evidence

Public screenshots of the live consent flow at https://getdeltaiq.com/. SMS is optional and notifies subscribers to sign in at My Account when their dashboard has been updated. Texts do not include market, ticker, price, or publication detail.

Direct image URLs:

  • https://getdeltaiq.com/sms-opt-in/consent-modal.png
  • https://getdeltaiq.com/sms-opt-in/homepage-consent.png

1. Subscribe consent modal (web-form opt-in)

Shown after Subscribe Now. Three checkboxes start unchecked. The third box is separate optional SMS consent for dashboard sign-in notifications.

2. Homepage pricing and public SMS disclosures

Offers section and the public Text notifications — optional SMS disclosures (#consent).

3. Welcome / confirmation SMS (sent once on opt-in)

Automated outbound text immediately after affirmative opt-in at checkout or in My Account. Includes program description, message frequency, Msg&data rates, STOP, and HELP.

DeltaIQ: Welcome! You'll receive occasional texts prompting you to sign in when new dashboard content is available. Frequency varies with publication activity. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Account: https://www.getdeltaiq.com/account

4. Publication notification SMS (recurring)

Dashboard call-to-action only — no market, ticker, price, or publication detail in the text.

DeltaIQ: New publication available. Sign in to view: https://www.getdeltaiq.com/account Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

5. Inbound STOP auto-reply

Subscriber texts STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, etc.). Opts out of texts only; subscription billing continues until cancel in My Account.

DeltaIQ: You're unsubscribed from texts. Subscription billing is unchanged — cancel anytime at https://www.getdeltaiq.com/account

6. Inbound HELP auto-reply

Subscriber texts HELP. Auto-reply includes review message and support contact; support also receives an email at support@getdeltaiq.com.

DeltaIQ: We are currently reviewing your question/concern. Dashboard sign-in alerts only. Support: support@getdeltaiq.com Account: https://www.getdeltaiq.com/account Reply STOP to opt out.

7. Inbound webhook (production)

Twilio POSTs inbound SMS to https://deltaiq-signal-engine-production.up.railway.app/api/twilio/inbound (configured on the sending long code).

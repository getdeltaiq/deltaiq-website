End users opt-in by visiting our website at https://getdeltaiq.com/ and navigating to the publication subscription page at https://getdeltaiq.com/sms-signup.html (the same digital form is also displayed on the homepage at https://getdeltaiq.com/#consent). They enter their phone number into a digital form and check an un-checked box explicitly agreeing to receive automated publication reminders via SMS from DeltaIQ. End-to-end opt-in flow (website form only)

  1. User visits https://getdeltaiq.com/ and navigates to the publication subscription page https://getdeltaiq.com/sms-signup.html (or the homepage form at #consent).
  2. User enters a mobile phone number into the digital form. The phone field is visible without login.
  3. User checks an unchecked box agreeing to receive automated publication reminders via SMS from DeltaIQ. Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links sit directly underneath the form.
  4. User continues to subscribe. Required Terms and Disclosures boxes (also unchecked) appear, then Stripe Checkout completes payment. SMS is not required to purchase.
  5. Alternatively, paid subscribers who declined SMS at signup may enable it later in My Account (login-gated; hosted replica below).

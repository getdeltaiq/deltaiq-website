DeltaIQ optional SMS opt-in evidence

Public documentation of the live web-form opt-in flow. SMS is optional, separate from purchase, and notifies subscribers to sign in at My Account when new dashboard content is available. Texts do not include market, ticker, price, or publication detail.

Legal pages (required disclosures)
  • Privacy Policy — mobile numbers not sold/shared for third-party marketing; message frequency; Msg&data rates may apply
  • Terms of Service — Section 5 SMS Notifications
  • Homepage SMS disclosures

End-to-end opt-in flow (web form only — no keyword/text-to-join)

  1. User visits https://getdeltaiq.com/ and clicks Subscribe Now.
  2. A consent modal appears (Step 1 below). Three checkboxes start unchecked. The third is optional SMS only. User may complete checkout without checking SMS.
  3. User continues to Stripe-hosted checkout (Step 2 below) and enters email, billing address, and mobile phone number. The submit line on Stripe reflects whether SMS was selected on the prior page (Msg&data rates, STOP/HELP).
  4. If SMS was selected, DeltaIQ stores ack_sms=true in checkout metadata and sends a one-time welcome SMS after successful checkout.
  5. Alternatively, paid subscribers who declined at checkout may enable SMS later in My Account (Step 3 below) with the same disclosures.

Step 2 — Stripe checkout (phone number collection)

After the modal, the user is redirected to Stripe Checkout (hosted on checkout.stripe.com). The left panel describes the subscription as a rules-based observational SPY/SPX dashboard publication; optional SMS is described as a sign-in notification only (no market, ticker, price, or publication detail in texts). Mobile phone number is collected on the right panel.

When the user selected optional SMS on getdeltaiq.com, the submit line states: “Start 14-day free trial (brand-new customers). You opted into optional SMS on getdeltaiq.com. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.” A separate Stripe checkbox requires agreement to Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (18+, US resident).

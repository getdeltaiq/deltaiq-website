DeltaIQ optional SMS opt-in evidence

Public screenshots of the live consent flow at https://getdeltaiq.com/. SMS is optional and notifies subscribers to sign in at My Account when their dashboard has been updated. Texts do not include market, ticker, price, or publication detail.

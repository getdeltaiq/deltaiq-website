End-to-end opt-in flow (web form only)

  1. User visits https://getdeltaiq.com/ and clicks Subscribe Now.
  2. A consent modal appears (Step 1). Three checkboxes start unchecked. The third is optional SMS only. User may complete checkout without checking SMS.
  3. User continues to Stripe-hosted checkout (Step 2) and enters email, billing address, and mobile phone number. The submit line reflects whether SMS was selected (Msg&data rates, STOP/HELP).
  4. If SMS was selected, DeltaIQ stores ack_sms=true in checkout metadata and sends a one-time welcome SMS after successful checkout.
  5. Alternatively, paid subscribers who declined at checkout may enable SMS later in My Account (Step 3) with the same disclosures.

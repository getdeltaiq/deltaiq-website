FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about the publication, dashboard, SMS, pricing, and account management.

What is DeltaIQ?

DeltaIQ is a rules-based publication service that monitors SPY and SPX market conditions and publishes observational analysis to subscribers on a shared Analytics Dashboard. It is informational and educational only — not personalized investment advice.

Is DeltaIQ investment advice?

No. DeltaIQ is a publisher of market-condition information. It is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or financial adviser. Publications do not recommend trades, sizes, or timing for any subscriber. See Important Disclosures.

Where do I read publications?

Full observational content appears on your subscriber Analytics Dashboard after you sign in through My Account. Optional SMS messages only notify you to sign in; they do not include ticker, price, or publication detail. See the Publication glossary.

Is SMS required?

No. SMS is optional and separate from purchase. You can subscribe and use the dashboard without texting. Opting out of SMS stops texts but does not by itself cancel billing.

Is there a free trial?

Brand-new customers are eligible for a free 14 calendar day trial before paid billing begins. Returning subscribers are not eligible for the trial; billing begins immediately.

How much does DeltaIQ cost?

Current list prices are $69/month or $690/year. The fee that controls is the amount shown at signup and checkout. See Offers.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime through My Account (Stripe). Cancellation is self-serve; we do not accept cancellation by email. Access continues through the end of the then-current billing period.

Who operates DeltaIQ?

DeltaIQ is operated by DeltaIQ, LLC and founded by Timothy Ong. Email support@getdeltaiq.com or visit Contact.

Overview

What is DeltaIQ

DeltaIQ is an automated, rules-based publication for traders who follow 0DTE SPY and SPX options. It monitors observable market conditions throughout the session and publishes identical observational content to all subscribers on the subscriber dashboard when those conditions are observed. Optional SMS can notify you to sign in when new content is available; full detail stays on the dashboard. Content is informational and educational — not personalized investment advice.

What it does

  • Applies a consistent, rules-based method to observable SPY/SPX conditions
  • Publishes identical observational content to all subscribers on the subscriber dashboard
  • Optionally sends a short text prompting you to sign in when new dashboard content is available

What it doesn't do

  • Does not provide personalized investment advice
  • Does not recommend trades, sizes, or timing for any subscriber
  • Does not create a client, advisory, or fiduciary relationship

Process

How it works

01

Subscribe

Sign up, accept the Terms and disclosures, and access the subscriber dashboard through My Account. Optionally opt in to text notifications when new content posts.

02

Conditions are observed

DeltaIQ evaluates market attributes on a fixed, automated cycle. When defined conditions are observed — including patterns that have historically coincided with notable SPY/SPX moves — the publication engine prepares an alert.

Alerts describe observed conditions and do not guarantee results.

03

Publication on your dashboard

When conditions are met, the full observational publication — price, conditions, and context — appears on your subscriber dashboard. All subscribers see the same content at the same time.

Optional text messages can notify you to sign in when new dashboard content is available. Texts are a call-to-action only; they do not include ticker, price, or condition detail.

My Account → Sign in to open your dashboard.

04

You interpret and decide

Each publication is observational. It describes current conditions for informational purposes. It is not a prediction, recommendation, or solicitation. You alone decide what, if anything, to do with the information.

Text notifications — optional SMS

SMS is optional and separate from purchase. You can subscribe and use the subscriber dashboard without texting. If you opt in (separate checkbox at checkout, unchecked by default), you may receive recurring automated texts that notify you when new subscriber dashboard content is available and prompt you to sign in at My Account. Text messages do not include market, ticker, price, or publication detail. Message frequency varies with how often we publish new dashboard content. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at support@getdeltaiq.com.
  • Declining SMS does not block checkout or dashboard access. Opting out of SMS stops texts but does not by itself cancel your subscription or billing. You can enable SMS later in My Account.

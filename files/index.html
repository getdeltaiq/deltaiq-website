Overview

What DeltaIQ is

An automated system that monitors intraday and next-session option market structure — 0DTE and 1DTE — in SPY and SPX. It applies the same rules every time, to everyone, simultaneously. When conditions meet the model’s definitions, a publication posts to your dashboard: the condition label and the live price at that instant.

The value is consistency. No human deciding when to speak up, no selective posting, no results reported after the fact. Every subscriber sees the identical publication at the identical moment.

What it does

  • Monitors SPY/SPX 0DTE and 1DTE market structure on a fixed automated cycle
  • Publishes the same market-condition read to every subscriber dashboard at the same time
  • Optionally texts you to sign in when new dashboard content is available (no market detail in SMS)

What DeltaIQ is not

  • It doesn’t tell you what to trade, when, at what strike, or in what size
  • It doesn’t know your account, risk tolerance, or goals
  • There is no advisory relationship and no fiduciary duty — what you do with the information is your decision

Product design

Why three lines

A DeltaIQ publication is intentionally short: a bull/bear condition label, the live SPY or SPX price, and a disclaimer. That minimalism is the product.

Most alert services sell you someone’s opinion dressed up as a signal — entries, stops, targets, and a story after the fact. DeltaIQ gives you the observation and gets out of the way, because the interpretation is the part you should own.

  • No narrative attached. The system does not invent a trade thesis for you.
  • No selective silence. When defined 0DTE/1DTE conditions occur, the same read publishes to everyone.
  • No after-the-fact performance theater. You see the condition and price at the moment of publication — then you decide.

If you want a feed that tells you what to buy, this is not it. If you want a disciplined, rules-based read on SPY/SPX market conditions without the Discord noise, that is exactly what DeltaIQ publishes. See the Publication glossary for the dashboard format.

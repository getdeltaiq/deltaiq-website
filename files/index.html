Overview

What is DeltaIQ

DeltaIQ is an educational tool for traders who follow 0DTE SPY and SPX options. It applies a consistent, rules-based method to monitor market conditions throughout the session and alerts you by SMS when a coil of attributes points to conditions historically associated with impactful price movement.

What it does

  • Monitors SPY/SPX conditions through a consistent, rules-based lens
  • Looks for coils — moments when multiple market attributes converge
  • Sends a plain-language SMS when those conditions are observed — no app required

What it doesn't do

  • Does not provide personalized investment advice
  • Alerts are observational and educational — not predictions or recommendations
  • You are responsible for your own trading decisions

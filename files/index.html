Overview

What is DeltaIQ

DeltaIQ is an automated, rules-based publication for traders who follow 0DTE SPY and SPX options. It monitors observable market conditions throughout the session and delivers the same plain-language SMS alert to all subscribers when those conditions are observed. Content is informational and educational — not personalized investment advice.

What it does

  • Applies a consistent, rules-based method to observable SPY/SPX conditions
  • Publishes identical alerts to all subscribers at the same time
  • Delivers plain-language SMS when defined conditions are observed — no app required

What it doesn't do

  • Does not provide personalized investment advice
  • Does not recommend trades, sizes, or timing for any subscriber
  • Does not create a client, advisory, or fiduciary relationship

Process

How it works

01

Subscribe

Sign up, accept the Terms and disclosures, and complete SMS opt-in.

02

Conditions are observed

DeltaIQ evaluates market attributes on a fixed, automated cycle. When defined conditions are observed — including patterns that have historically coincided with notable SPY/SPX moves — the publication engine prepares an alert.

Alerts describe observed conditions and do not guarantee results.

03

You interpret and decide

Each alert is observational. It describes current conditions for informational purposes. It is not a prediction, recommendation, or solicitation. You alone decide what, if anything, to do with the information.

By subscribing to DeltaIQ, you consent to receive recurring automated SMS alerts from DeltaIQ related to published market-condition observations. Message frequency varies based on market activity. Message and data rates may apply.
